Closing Carl Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email rjr5021@gmail.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the game (it adds your game version). We usually answer within two days.

Common questions

How do I move up to the next office? Get every desk item and every stage in your office to level 25. Tap the office name at the top to see how close you are. Hold Best Buy to keep upgrading.

Why isn't my income going up? The slowest stage in the pipeline (shown in red) sets the pace. Upgrade that stage, or hire staff for it.

I bought something and didn't get it. Open the Gold Shop or Settings and tap Restore purchases. Purchases that need a parent's approval arrive as soon as they're approved.

I got a new phone. VIP and the Starter Pack come back with Restore purchases. To bring your whole game, sign in with Apple in Settings → Cloud save on the old phone first.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Delete account deletes your cloud save and account. Your game on the phone is deleted with the app.

Closing Carl is a game. It is not financial advice, and every lender, bank, client and brand in it is made up.

Ayuda de Closing Carl

¿Preguntas, un error o una idea? Escribe a rjr5021@gmail.com o usa Ajustes → Enviar comentarios en el juego.

¿Cómo me mudo a la siguiente oficina? Sube cada objeto de los escritorios y cada etapa al nivel 25. Toca el nombre de la oficina arriba para ver cuánto te falta.

¿Por qué no suben mis ingresos? La etapa más lenta (en rojo) marca el ritmo. Mejórala o contrata personal para ella.

Compré algo y no me llegó. Abre la Tienda de oro o Ajustes y toca Restaurar compras.

¿Cómo borro mis datos? Ajustes → Borrar la cuenta borra tu partida en la nube y la cuenta.

Closing Carl es un juego, no un consejo financiero.
