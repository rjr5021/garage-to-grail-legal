Arcade Inc. Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email rjr5021@gmail.com, or use Settings → Contact support in the game. We usually answer within two days.

Common questions

How do I move to a bigger arcade? Fill three quarters of your spots with Working cabinets and save up the lease. Tap the place name at the top to see what's needed.

How do I play a cabinet? Tap it on the floor, then Play. It has to be at least Flickering. Beat the house #1 score to make it HOT.

What are the odds in a Sealed Lot? Tap Odds next to any pallet, liquidation or Sealed Lot in the Get sheet. Every lot has a Color Variant or better, and a Prototype or better comes within 20 lots.

I bought something and didn't get it. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Purchases that need a parent's approval arrive as soon as they're approved.

I got a new phone. VIP, the Starter Pack and the Neon Nights Pass come back with Restore purchases. To bring your whole arcade, sign in with Apple in Settings → Cloud save on the old phone first.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Delete account deletes your cloud save and account. Your game on the phone is deleted with the app.

Ayuda de Arcade Inc.

¿Preguntas, un error o una idea? Escribe a rjr5021@gmail.com o usa Ajustes → Contactar con soporte en el juego.

¿Cómo me mudo a un arcade más grande? Llena tres cuartas partes de tus lugares con máquinas que funcionen y ahorra para el alquiler. Toca el nombre del lugar arriba para ver qué te falta.

¿Qué probabilidades tiene un Lote Sellado? Toca Probabilidades junto a cualquier palé, liquidación o Lote Sellado.

Compré algo y no me llegó. Abre Ajustes y toca Restaurar compras.

¿Cómo borro mis datos? Ajustes → Borrar cuenta borra tu partida en la nube y la cuenta.
