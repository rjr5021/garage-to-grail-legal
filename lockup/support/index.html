Common questions

How do I get my purchases back on a new phone?
Open Settings (the gear) → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. VIP and the Starter Pack's jumpsuit come back; Gold Keys you already spent don't.

A purchase says "Check" or "Waiting for approval".
The App Store hasn't confirmed it yet (for example, Ask to Buy is waiting for a parent). It arrives on its own as soon as it's approved; tap Check to ask the App Store again.

How do I keep my prison if I change phones?
Settings → Cloud save → Sign in with Apple. On the new phone, sign in the same way and pick the cloud save.

How do I delete my cloud save and account?
Settings → Delete account. The prison on your phone stays.

What are the odds for Recruit draws?
Open Recruit in the game: every star staff tier and its chance is listed above the draw buttons, along with the guarantee (Epic or better within 20 draws). Paid draws are not available in Belgium or the Netherlands.

I saw an ad that was wrong or offensive.
Settings → Report an ad, and tell us roughly when you saw it.

The game is in the wrong language.
Settings → Language: Phone, English or Español.
